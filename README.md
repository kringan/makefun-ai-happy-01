# AI Happy — golden retriever puppy in autumn leaves

A golden retriever puppy mid-leap through a burst of golden autumn
leaves, tongue out, eyes sparkling — one specific moment of pure delight
in warm golden-hour light. The program copies the bundled image to
/output/entry.png and validates it (whole JPEG, under 1 MiB, within
1024x1024, byte-identical every run).
